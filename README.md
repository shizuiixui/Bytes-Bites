#  Bytes & Bites

> An offline-first, privacy-focused mobile application for college students to track their monthly budget and dorm pantry inventory in one secure place.

##  About The Project

Managing finances and groceries as a student living in a boarding house can be overwhelming. **Bytes & Bites** solves this by combining two essential trackers into a single, intuitive dashboard. 

* **Bytes:** Track your expenses, allowances, and upcoming subscriptions.
* **Bites:** Manage your dorm pantry, adjust quantities with a single tap, and get low-stock alerts.

Built with an absolute focus on user privacy, the app requires no account creation, connects to no cloud servers, and stores all data 100% locally on the device, protected by native biometric security.

##  Key Features

* **Unified Dashboard:** See your total spending and critical low-stock pantry items on a single screen.
* **Lightning-Fast Inventory:** Update your pantry stock directly from the list view with a single tap (no deep menus).
* **Biometric & PIN Security:** Lock your financial data behind Face ID, Touch ID, or a custom 4-digit PIN.
* **Offline-First Architecture:** No internet connection required. Your data never leaves your phone.
* **Categorized Expense Tracking:** Keep track of rent, food, and subscriptions with visual indicators.


##  Built With

* **Framework:** [Flutter](https://flutter.dev/)
* **Language:** Dart
* **Local Storage:** `shared_preferences`
* **Security:** `local_auth`, `flutter_secure_storage`
* **Design:** Figma

##  Privacy Policy

Bytes & Bites operates strictly locally. We do not collect, harvest, or transmit any personal data, financial records, or biometric information to external servers. All information is encrypted and stored safely on the user's local device hardware.
